Data Lineage & Expected Columns

Each row in the dataset represents an auditor's monthly performance for a specific account.

  • Year – Reporting year.
  • Month – Month label (e.g., Jan-24).
  • Account Abbreviation – Account code.
  • Auditor – Name of the auditor.
  • Qualification Status – Qualified / Not Qualified.
  • Error – Qualified – Errors by Qualified auditors.
  • Error – Not Qualified – Errors by Not Qualified auditors.
  • Total Errors – All errors combined.
  • FTE – Qualified – Audited FTE for Qualified auditors.
  • FTE – Not Qualified – Audited FTE for Not Qualified.
  • Total Audited FTE – Combined FTE.
  • Average Working Days – Avg. calendar working days for the selected period.

Business Logic & Formulas

Error Counts

  • Error – Qualified = Total errors by Qualified auditors
  • Error – Not Qualified = Total errors by Not Qualified auditors
  • Total Errors = Qualified Errors + Not Qualified Errors

Audited FTE

Audited effort is converted into FTE using the following denominator:

Total Audit Minutes = If (Audited # = 0 , Audit Time , Audited # * Average Processing time)

Audited FTE = Total Audit Minutes ÷ (7 × 60 × (235/250) × Working Days)

Error per Hour (Error Rate)

Indicates errors per hour of audited work.

Error per Hour = Total Errors ÷ (Audited FTE × Working Days × 7)

Overall Error Rate

Overall Error per Hour = Total Errors ÷ (Total Audited FTE × Working Days × 7)

Reading the Trend Chart

  • Trend Line: Represents KPI changes month over month.
  • Markers: Points show exact month values.
  • Labels: Display computed KPI values.
  • Filters: All results adjust instantly based on selections.
