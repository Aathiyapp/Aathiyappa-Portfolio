Total Quality System – User Guide

Getting Started

Open the following file to launch the portal:

X:\4 - Team Leaders\10 - OPEX\TQS Reports\4- Dashboard\2026\Quality Management Portal.html

On the home page, click the TQS card to open the Total Quality System dashboard.

Navigation

  1. Use the TQS card on the home page to enter the dashboard.
  2. Use the top filters (Year, Account Manager, Supervisor, Team Leader, Account) to refine the scope. The page updates instantly.
  3. Click a KPI card on the left to switch the main trend chart to that metric.
  4. Use the data table at the bottom to search, sort, and review detailed account level data.
  5. Click Export to Excel to download the current, filtered view.
Filter dropdowns automatically adapt to your selections and only show valid options.

KPI Cards

KPIDescription
Total Billed FTE Total Full-Time Equivalent billed within the selected scope.
Client Found Errors Total errors identified by the client.
Internal Errors Total errors identified in internal audit.
Client Found Error per FTE Client-found errors relative to billed FTE.
Error per Hour Errors per audit hour.
FTE Audited % Percent of production audited.
FTE Meet Compliance % Extent to which the audit plan was followed.
FTE Meeting TQL % Percent of audited volume meeting TQL standards.
Audit Time per Audited FTE (Minutes) Average audit time per audited FTE.

Metric Formulas

These definitions reflect the underlying business formulas used to derive the KPIs.

Client Reported Error per Billed FTE

Formula: Total Client-Reported Errors ÷ FTE Billed

Error per Hour

Formula: Total Errors ÷ (Audited FTE × Calendar Working Days × 7 hours)

Percentage FTE Audited

Formula: Total Audited FTE ÷ Total FTE Work Produced

Percentage FTE Meet Compliance

Formula: Compliant FTE ÷ Total FTE

Percentage FTE Volume Meeting TQL

Formula: FTE Meeting TQL ÷ Total Audited FTE

Audit Time / FTE / Day (Minutes)

Formula: Total Audit Time (Qualified only) ÷ (Total Charged FTE × Calendar Working Days × (235/250))

Reading the Trend Chart

  • Trend line: monthly progression of the selected KPI.
  • Dashed target line: the configured target, where applicable.
  • Colored regions: visual cue of performance against the target.
  • Value labels: exact values shown near each data point.
